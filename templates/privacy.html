Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Data We Collect

When you create a Spendly account we collect your name, email address, and a securely hashed version of your password.

As you use the Service we store the expense records you enter — amounts, categories, dates, and any descriptions or notes you add. We also keep basic technical logs, such as your browser type and the time of requests, to help us keep the Service running.

How We Use Your Data

We use your information solely to provide and improve the Service: to authenticate you, to display and summarise your expenses, and to respond to support requests.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use your expense records for advertising.

Data Storage

Your data is stored in a database on servers operated on our behalf. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

You can delete individual expenses or your entire account at any time. When you delete your account, your records are removed from the active database, though copies may persist in encrypted backups for a limited period before being overwritten.

Third Party Services

Spendly relies on a small number of third party providers for hosting, database storage, and email delivery. These providers process data only as needed to perform their services for us and are bound by their own confidentiality and security obligations.

We do not share your information with third parties for any purpose other than operating the Service, unless required to do so by law.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your data is handled, contact us at privacy@spendly.example.

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above.
